# QuickCrate

Fill your Waylaid Crates and apply Forged Delivery Receipts with one click each.

QuickCrate opens a small window listing every Waylaid Crate in your bags. For each crate it shows what it will fill it with, and Fill does the rest: it opens the crate and puts the right materials in, without you hunting through your bags.

## Features

- **One-click Fill.** Each crate row says what it fills with, for example "Fills with 20× Tin Ore". Click Fill and QuickCrate uses the crate and puts the material in.
- **Picks the materials for you.** When a crate accepts more than one bundle, QuickCrate chooses the first one your bags can cover.
- **Shows what's missing.** If your bags can't cover any bundle, the row says what you're short of, and Fill is greyed out until you have it.
- **Forged Delivery Receipts.** With receipts in your bags, every crate row gets a Receipt button that puts one receipt on that crate.
- **All your crates at a glance.** Several crates of the same kind share one row with a count, and crates are found in every bag, including the reagent bag.
- **Five skins.** Metal (the default), Portrait, Parchment, Slate and Plain.

## How to use it

- Right-click a Waylaid Crate or a Forged Delivery Receipt in your bags, and the window opens by itself.
- Or type **/qc** (or **/quickcrate**) to open and close it.
- Click **Fill** on a crate row to fill that crate. If the crate takes a moment before asking for the material, the button changes to **Put in**: click it once more to finish.
- Click **Receipt** on a crate row to apply a Forged Delivery Receipt to that crate.
- Crates QuickCrate doesn't recognise are listed as **Fill by hand**.
- Drag the window to move it. It remembers where you left it.
- Press **Esc** or click the **X** to close it.

## Skins

Click the cogwheel next to the close button to open the settings, then pick a skin from the dropdown. The window changes straight away, and your choice is remembered for every character.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/qc` or `/quickcrate` | Opens or closes the window |
| `/qc debug` | Lists every Waylaid item and receipt in your bags, which helps when reporting a problem |

Report bugs and suggestions on the project's [GitHub issues page](https://github.com/theliahh/QuickCrate/issues). For a crate QuickCrate gets wrong, include the output of `/qc debug`.
