# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release of QuickCrate: one-click filling of Waylaid Crates and applying Forged Delivery Receipts.
- Skins: Metal (the default), Portrait, Parchment, Slate and Plain, chosen from a settings window opened by the cogwheel in the title bar.
- The settings window has its own close button, and Esc closes QuickCrate.
