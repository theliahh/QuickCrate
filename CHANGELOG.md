# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Fill no longer fails with "not enough" when the material is split across stacks, such as a 4-strut leftover next to a full stack. It now fills from a stack big enough for the crate.
- When you have enough of a material but only in small stacks, the button reads Stack: one click combines them so the crate can be filled.

## 0.1.0

- First release of QuickCrate: one-click filling of Waylaid Crates and applying Forged Delivery Receipts.
- Skins: Metal (the default), Portrait, Parchment, Slate and Plain, chosen from a settings window opened by the cogwheel in the title bar.
- The settings window has its own close button, and Esc closes QuickCrate.
